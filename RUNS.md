# Run ledger

Your logbook. One line per run, appended, never edited. The verdict goes in **before**
reality has answered; the outcome column is filled in later, whenever it does.

Why append-only: the classic self-deception is a case that goes KILL, gets quietly rewritten,
and comes back ADVANCE. In a ledger that only grows, that shows up as exactly that.
